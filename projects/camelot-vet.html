Camelot Vet Services

challenge

Camelot had 252 Google reviews and a 4.7-star rating but no website. More urgently, their Weave patient texting account was blocked from SMS verification under 10DLC rules until a live, compliant site existed — meaning they couldn't reach patients by text at all.

solution

content strategy

Researched local veterinary clinics to understand how competitors positioned themselves and identify opportunities for Camelot to stand out. Wrote original copy for the Home, About, and Services pages, creating a warm, clear, and trustworthy voice that speaks directly to pet owners.

compliance

Wrote and published the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy required for SMS verification compliance. Configured local SEO, including page titles, meta descriptions, and sitemap settings, to help nearby pet owners discover the clinic through search.

design

Designed and built the complete website in Squarespace, focusing on a clean, professional appearance and intuitive navigation. Structured the site so the Camelot team could easily manage content and make updates independently.
