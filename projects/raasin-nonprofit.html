Raasin in the Sun

Nonprofit Brand Narrative | Client Project | 2025 - 2026

challenge

RITS had significant real-world credibility — co-leading the $112M Block 16 & 18 cultural corridor, restoring the home of Austin's first African American elected official — but their digital presence didn't reflect that scale. The site needed to serve two very different audiences: community members and institutional grant funders.

View Live Site →

solution

custom

Designed and built five custom pages and redesigned five existing pages, creating a cohesive, polished website that reflects Raasin in the Sun's identity. Focused on consistent layouts, clear navigation, and engaging visuals throughout the site.

storytelling

Wrote original website copy in Raasin in the Sun's warm, community-rooted voice. Redesigned the About page to introduce the founder, highlight the team, and communicate the organization's mission and programs.

community

Created dedicated pages for Block 16 & 18, Music Lane, and Seabrook Square, bringing each community project to life through thoughtful layouts and visual storytelling. Organized project histories, artist spotlights, photography, drone footage, and descriptions to showcase the people, places, and stories behind the work.
